> Your movies and shows are already organized — folders with `.nfo` files and cover art, scraped by Kodi or MediaBrowser over the years. MediaDroid plays that library straight from local storage on any Android box, tablet, or phone: no server, no account, no re-scraping, no re-encoding. It reads what is already on disk and turns it into a browse-and-play app simple enough for a six-year-old and a grandparent.

![The browse wall](screenshots/main.png)

## 🧭 Vision

Media libraries on Android are usually either a raw file browser or a client of someone else's server. MediaDroid claims the middle ground that home media collectors actually live in: the folders are the library, and the `.nfo` sidecars and `folder.jpg` covers you already scraped are the metadata — no database of ours in between. The bar for success is a household where nobody has to be taught the app: a kid finds a film by its picture, a grandparent resumes a show where they left off, and everything answers instantly even on cheap hardware. The parser, the scanner, the search and the binge logic are pure and tested; the screens sit on top of them and must never get slower than the hardware allows. The first working build scans a library, shows the wall, plays with resume and binge auto-advance — polish and per-device codec tuning come next.

## ✨ Features

- Reads `movie.nfo`, `tvshow.nfo`, and per-episode NFOs: plot, cast, runtime, rating, sets, trailer links.
- Covers from `folder.jpg` / `poster.jpg`, backdrops from `fanart.jpg`, logos and banners where present.
- Binge mode: when an episode ends, the next unwatched one starts — season by season, in order.
- Resume: every item remembers where you stopped.
- Instant search across the whole library.
- Filter by genre and year, group by decade, facet counts as you browse.
- Big touch targets and high contrast — usable by kids and older users without glasses-or-guessing.
- Fully offline: no account, no telemetry, nothing leaves the device.

## 📦 Installation

Android 8.0 (API 26) or newer, phone, tablet, or TV box. Not on the Play Store (yet): download the APK from [Releases](https://github.com/Hawkynt/MediaDroid/releases) — a dated stable build or a `nightly-*` build — and enable installation from unknown sources. Storage stays on your device; the app needs access only to the folders you point it at.

## 🚀 Quick start

```bash
adb install app-debug.apk
```

Open MediaDroid, grant file access, point it at the root of your media folders and hit Scan: the wall fills with covers, and plots, seasons and episode thumbs come from the `.nfo` files already sitting there. Tapping a show offers Play next — episodes run in order and mark themselves watched.

## 🛠️ Building

Requires JDK 21 and an Android SDK with platform 37; `local.properties` must point `sdk.dir` at it.

```bash
./gradlew assembleDebug   # app/build/outputs/apk/debug/app-debug.apk
./gradlew test            # parser, library, and binge-logic unit tests
```
