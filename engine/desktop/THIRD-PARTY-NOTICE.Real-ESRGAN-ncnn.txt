Real-ESRGAN on ncnn, by Xintao Wang and others

engine/desktop/src/main/resources/models/realesr-animevideov3-x4/ holds realesr-animevideov3-x4.param and .bin: the
realesr-animevideov3 network (SRVGGNetCompact, https://github.com/xinntao/Real-ESRGAN) as Real-ESRGAN-ncnn-vulkan
(https://github.com/xinntao/Real-ESRGAN-ncnn-vulkan) converts and ships it for ncnn — taken unchanged from
realesrgan-ncnn-vulkan-20220424-ubuntu.zip (https://github.com/xinntao/Real-ESRGAN/releases/tag/v0.2.5.0, sha256
e5aa6eb131234b87c0c51f82b89390f5e3e642b7b70f2b9bbe95b6a285a40c96), its models/ folder. native/src/enlarger.cpp runs it
as that port does: a prepadding of 10, mirrored, the output four times the size.
