FFmpeg through Bytedeco's JavaCPP presets

The desktop engine (engine/desktop) decodes with FFmpeg 8.1 (https://ffmpeg.org) through Bytedeco's JavaCPP and its
FFmpeg presets (https://github.com/bytedeco/javacpp-presets, Apache License 2.0), taken unchanged from Maven Central
(org.bytedeco:javacpp 1.5.14, org.bytedeco:ffmpeg 8.1.2-1.5.14).

Published builds ship FFmpeg's LGPL build (GNU Lesser General Public License, version 3 or later): its natives are
the separate shared libraries inside the ffmpeg-*-<platform>.jar, which can be replaced by any compatible build.
The GPL build (classifier "-gpl") is used only on the household's own machines (the Gradle property fullFfmpeg)
and is never distributed.
