DAIN, by Wenbo Bao and others (https://github.com/baowenbo/DAIN; the paper: Depth-Aware Video Frame Interpolation,
CVPR 2019), as ncnn runs it: dain-ncnn-vulkan, by nihui (https://github.com/nihui/dain-ncnn-vulkan).

The model is not in the app: switched on in Settings, it is fetched from huggingface.co/Hawkynt/mediadroid-models
(dain/), where dain-ncnn-vulkan's models/best files lie unchanged, each file checked against its SHA-256 digest (see
core/player/.../NeuralModels.kt). The layers the model calls — dain.Correlation, dain.OpticalFlowWarp,
dain.DepthFlowProjection, dain.FilterInterpolation (native/src/dain_layers.cpp and
stage/src/main/glsl/ncnn/dain-*.comp) — are converted from dain-ncnn-vulkan's, and native/src/networks.cpp runs
the four networks the way its DAIN::process_notile does.
