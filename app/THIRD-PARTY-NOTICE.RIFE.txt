RIFE 4.25 lite, by hzwer and others (https://github.com/hzwer/Practical-RIFE; the paper: RIFE, ECCV 2022)

app/src/main/assets/models/rife-4.25-lite-576p.tflite is the 4.25.lite model (flownet.pkl, released under the
same MIT licence as Practical-RIFE), converted to TensorFlow Lite at 768 x 640 for the in-between at t = 0.5 by
tools/rife: the network written out from its published architecture, the weights loaded as tensors only, exported
through ONNX with onnx2tf. Checked against PyTorch on test frames: mean difference 0.00008, 0.08 % of values off
by more than 1/255.
