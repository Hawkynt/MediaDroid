RIFE 4.25 lite, by hzwer and others (https://github.com/hzwer/Practical-RIFE; the paper: RIFE, ECCV 2022), as
ncnn runs it: rife-ncnn-vulkan, by nihui (https://github.com/nihui/rife-ncnn-vulkan), in TNTwise's continuation
(https://github.com/TNTwise/rife-ncnn-vulkan) that carries the 4.x models.

app/src/main/assets/models/rife-4.25-lite/flownet.param and flownet.bin are TNTwise's conversion of the 4.25.lite
model (flownet.pkl, released under the same MIT licence as Practical-RIFE), copied unchanged. The warp layer the
model calls (rife.Warp: app/src/main/cpp/warp_layer.cpp and stage/src/main/glsl/ncnn/warp.comp) is converted from
rife-ncnn-vulkan's, and app/src/main/cpp/interpolator.cpp runs the model the way its RIFE::process_v4 does — the
frames in 0..1, padded with zeros to a multiple of 128, the in-between's time as a plane.
