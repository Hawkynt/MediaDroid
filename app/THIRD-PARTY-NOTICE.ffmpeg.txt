libs/lib-decoder-ffmpeg-release.aar

Built from the FFmpeg decoder extension module of androidx/media release 1.11.1
(https://github.com/androidx/media, Apache License 2.0), statically linking
FFmpeg 6.0 (https://ffmpeg.org, GNU LGPL version 3 or later) configured with
only the following decoders enabled:

  h264 hevc mpeg2video vp8 vp9 av1 ac3 eac3 dts truehd mlp alac vorbis opus flac

No GPL-licensed FFmpeg component is configured or linked. The LGPL conditions
(linked as a library, replaceable, source obtainable from upstream) are met;
the exact build recipe lives in tools/ffmpeg/README.md.
