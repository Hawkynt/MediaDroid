libs/lib-decoder-ffmpeg-release.aar

Built by tools/ffmpeg/build.sh from these sources, and nothing else:

- The FFmpeg decoder extension module of androidx/media release 1.11.1
  (https://github.com/androidx/media), Apache License 2.0.

- Its FFmpeg video renderer from androidx/media pull request #1591
  "Implement an FfmpegVideoRenderer" by rabbitknight
  (https://github.com/androidx/media/pull/1591), contributed under the
  Apache License 2.0 — tools/ffmpeg/patches/0001-*.patch, applied unchanged.

- MediaDroid's own demuxer binding and codec-parameter handover
  (tools/ffmpeg/mediadroid/, tools/ffmpeg/patches/0002-*.patch),
  GNU LGPL version 3 or later.

- libyuv (https://chromium.googlesource.com/libyuv/libyuv), BSD 3-Clause
  License, Copyright 2011 The LibYuv Project Authors; statically linked.

- FFmpeg 6.0 (https://ffmpeg.org), GNU LGPL version 2.1 or later; libavformat,
  libavcodec, libswscale, libswresample and libavutil, statically linked.
  Configured --disable-everything, then only the decoders, demuxers and parsers
  listed in tools/ffmpeg/build.sh; no protocols, no encoders, no muxers.

No GPL-licensed FFmpeg component is configured or linked. The LGPL conditions
(linked as a library, replaceable, source obtainable from upstream) are met;
the exact build recipe lives in tools/ffmpeg/README.md.
