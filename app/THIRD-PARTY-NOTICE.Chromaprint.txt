Chromaprint (https://github.com/acoustid/chromaprint, revision 3b38c100022bdfb49be7797c4828a476f1621d55), with the
KISS FFT and the FFmpeg audio resampler it carries

The native tree (native/chromaprint) builds Chromaprint from its source — which native/build.gradle.kts fetches and
checks against its digest — into MediaDroyd's native library (libmediadroyd_neural.so in the app, the mediadroyd
library on the desktop), unmodified, to fingerprint the sound of episodes and find their intros and credits.

  - Chromaprint's own code: MIT License, Copyright (C) 2010-2016 Lukas Lalinsky.
  - KISS FFT (src/3rdparty/kissfft): BSD 3-Clause License, Copyright (c) 2003-2010 Mark Borgerding.
  - src/avresample/resample2.c, from FFmpeg: GNU Lesser General Public License, version 2.1 or later, Copyright (c)
    2004 Michael Niedermayer. It is linked statically; MediaDroyd's own source (LGPL-3.0-or-later) and the build that
    links it are published, so it can be rebuilt against a changed resampler.

MediaDroyd's matcher (core/player PrintMatcher) is converted from MediaUploadService's port
(https://github.com/Hawkynt/MediaUploadService, LGPL-3.0, the same author) of Chromaprint's FingerprintMatcher::Match
(src/fingerprint_matcher.cpp, src/utils/gaussian_filter.h, src/utils/gradient.h), under Chromaprint's MIT License.
