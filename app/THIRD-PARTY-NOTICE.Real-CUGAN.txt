Real-CUGAN, by bilibili (https://github.com/bilibili/ailab/tree/main/Real-CUGAN), as ncnn runs it:
realcugan-ncnn-vulkan, by nihui (https://github.com/nihui/realcugan-ncnn-vulkan).

app/src/main/assets/models/realcugan-se holds realcugan-ncnn-vulkan's models-se/up2x-conservative (the SE model,
conservative, twice the size), copied unchanged. Its other models — models-se at 2x (no denoise, denoise 1x to 3x),
3x and 4x, and models-pro at 2x and 3x — are fetched when picked, unchanged, from the port's own repository at commit
395302c5c70f1bff604c974e92e0a87e45c9f9ee, each file checked against its digest. native/src/enlarger.cpp runs them the
way the port does: in RGB 0..1, the edges mirrored — at 2x 18 around and to an even size, at 3x 14 around and to a
multiple of four, at 4x 19 around and to an even size, the network's output added to the picture's pixels repeated
four times — the output's top left the picture enlarged; in tiles and eight ways (TTA) as the port does them.
