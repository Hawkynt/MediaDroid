XVFI: eXtreme Video Frame Interpolation (ICCV 2021), by Hyeonjun Sim, Jihyong Oh and Munchurl Kim
(https://github.com/JihyongOh/XVFI).

Its terms, from the XVFI repository's README:

    The source codes and datasets can be freely used for research and education only. Any commercial use should get
    formal permission first.

MediaDroid uses XVFI with its authors' permission, under those terms: free, never sold. The network is not in the
app: switched on, it is fetched from huggingface.co/Hawkynt/mediadroid-models (xvfi/, with LICENSE-XVFI), each file
checked against its SHA-256 digest (core/player/.../NeuralModels.kt) — the authors' Vimeo-trained weights
(XVFInet_Vimeo_exp1_latest.pt) as tools/xvfi/export.py writes its convolution blocks out for ncnn, from
tools/xvfi/xvfi_net.py, the network written out from its published architecture. native/src/xvfi.cpp joins the
blocks as xvfi_net.py's forward does. Anyone taking MediaDroid's source takes these terms with the XVFI parts: they
are not covered by MediaDroid's own licence.
